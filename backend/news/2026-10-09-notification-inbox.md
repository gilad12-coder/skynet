---
title: Notifications now have an inbox
date: 2026-10-09
link: /?settings=notifications
---
Everything your runs tell you now lands in one inbox under the bell. Focused shows what needs you: runs waiting for input, runs near their spending limit, and runs that finished or failed. Everything also shows new best scores, stalled runs, shares and product news like this one.

Choose where each kind of event reaches you in Settings, Notifications. Email waits a few minutes before it goes out, and is skipped when you already read the item here. Progress and digest emails are gone.
